# Zerodha AI Financial Intelligence Platform - Video Demo Script

## Duration: ~3 to 5 Minutes
**Objective**: Demonstrate end-to-end intelligence workflow from portfolio ingestion to MCP unification, deterministic analytics, Gemini explainability, recommendation console, and internal operations/compliance review.

---

### Step 1: Introduction & Problem Statement (0:00 - 0:45)
- Open the application on the **Stocks & Indices** tab.
- Explain: *"Retail investors see numbers and price changes on brokerage screens, but lack context on why their portfolio moved, where risk is concentrated, and what next-best review steps to take."*
- Highlight the 6 primary product tabs: Stocks, F&O & IPOs, Mutual Funds, Portfolio AI Hub, Operations & MCP, and Compliance Audit.

### Step 2: Portfolio CSV Ingestion & Authentication (0:45 - 1:30)
- Sign in or upload a sample Zerodha Kite CSV file via the **+ Upload File** modal.
- Show the instantaneous normalization of symbols, sector mapping, and PostgreSQL persistence.

### Step 3: Portfolio AI Hub & Deterministic Analytics (1:30 - 2:45)
- Switch to the **Portfolio AI Hub** tab.
- Walk through:
  1. **Dynamic Benchmark Relative Performance Card** (NIFTY 50 vs. Portfolio Alpha).
  2. **Executive Summary** generated by Gemini 1.5/3.5 Flash.
  3. **Risk & Exposure Panel** showing >40% Technology Concentration alert, Maximum Drawdown (-14.2%), and Annualized Volatility (24.8%).
  4. **Market Movement Explainer**: Stock-level contributors (TCS, Infosys) linked to recent corporate news.
  5. **Explainable Recommendation Console**: 4 reviewable card categories (Risk Attention, Diversification Review, Catalyst Monitoring, Rebalancing Follow-up) with confidence score & disclaimers.

### Step 4: Governed MCP Server & Live Operations (2:45 - 3:45)
- Switch to the **Operations & MCP** tab.
- Demonstrate:
  - System Uptime, error rate, p50/p95 latency metrics.
  - Active MCP tool execution distribution.
  - Interactive MCP Tool Console: Select `fetch_market_quotes` or `compute_portfolio_metrics`, hit **Execute MCP Tool**, and showcase the governed JSON response.

### Step 5: Compliance & Regulatory Governance (3:45 - 4:30)
- Switch to the **Compliance Audit** tab.
- Showcase:
  - Cryptographic SHA-256 prompt hashing and model version traceability.
  - Factual Grounding and Non-Advice regulatory compliance rules.
  - Demonstrate a Compliance Officer sign-off (`APPROVED` / `FLAGGED`) with reviewer notes.

### Step 6: Summary & Closing (4:30 - 5:00)
- Reiterate that this platform delivers transparent, auditable portfolio intelligence without unmanaged trade-execution risk.
